Raw = average of 12 ADC reads, 0-4095. Moisture = relative to your D (dry) and W (wet) references, 0-100%.

Calibration from raw values

Board DRYBoard WETLast 30 sNew DRYNew WETNow as %
How the board turns raw counts into %: pct = 100 × (raw − dry) / (wet − dry), clamped to 0-100. Capacitive probes usually read HIGHER when dry. Leave the probes still in dry soil, wait until "settled", press "Use as DRY"; then the same in wet soil. Sending stores both on the board and arms AUTO.

The board replaces the three sensor readings with these raw values for up to 5 minutes. The filter, thresholds, pulses, soak time and lockout all run as normal, so you can check the pump at 45% or 75% without drying or flooding the soil.
